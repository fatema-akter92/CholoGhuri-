Verified Travel Advisories & Regional Restrictions

Saint Martin's Island & Bay Ferries: Ships run mainly November–March subject to BIWTA sea-state clearance & conservation daily caps.
Chattogram Hill Tracts (Sajek, Bandarban, Rangamati): Carry original NID/Passport at checkpoints. Foreign passport holders require advance DC/Home Ministry approval for restricted Bandarban frontiers.
Sundarbans Mangrove Reserve: Forest Department entry permit and licensed forest guards are mandatory; closed June–August during wildlife breeding season.
